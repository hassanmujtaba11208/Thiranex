My story

I'm a Software Engineering student with a strong interest in building practical, modern, and user-friendly software applications. My coursework has taken me across the stack — from structuring databases and designing APIs to shaping the interfaces people actually interact with.

I'm particularly drawn to full-stack development, where a project comes together end to end: a clean frontend, a dependable backend, and infrastructure that can support it in production. Alongside that, I've been exploring how AI-assisted features — like OCR, analysis, and automated insights — can make software genuinely more useful, not just more complex.

Cloud technologies are another growing focus. Understanding how to deploy, host, and scale an application is, to me, just as important as writing the application itself. I enjoy the process of taking an idea from a rough plan to a working, deployed system.
